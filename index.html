Common questions

Things parents & new residents ask.

Don't see your question? Drop us a message.

Is the hostel only for girls?

Yes — Annie is an all-girls residence. Male visitors are received only in the front common area, with prior notice and only during visiting hours.

What's included in the monthly rent?

Three meals a day, Wi-Fi, electricity (within fair-use), water, ironing facility, and security. Laundry is twice-a-week, included.

Can my parents visit before I book?

Absolutely — we encourage it. Schedule a visit through the booking form and our warden will give you a tour of the building, kitchen, and rooms.

What's the curfew & visitor policy?

Gate closes at 10:00 pm with sign-out flexibility for evening classes and emergencies. Visitors are received in the common area between 10am – 7pm.

How do I pay rent?

Bank transfer, EasyPaisa, or cash at the office. Rent is paid by the 5th of each month; receipts are issued for every payment.
